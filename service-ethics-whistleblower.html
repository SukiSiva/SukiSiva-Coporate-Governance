Integrity & Ethics Practice

Ethics & Whistleblower Frameworks Practice

Multi-channel anonymous reporting channels, anti-bribery / FCPA compliance systems, escalation procedures, and investigation governance.

Practice Overview & Institutional Culture

Whistleblower allegations concerning executive misconduct, accounting fraud, or anti-bribery violations (FCPA/UKBA) demand immediate, defensible handling. If mishandled, allegations leak to regulatory authorities or investigative media. Governora designs confidential intake channels, privileged escalation trees, and board investigation governance protocols.

Key Boardroom Challenges Addressed

Executive Interference in Reporting Pipelines

Serious allegations buried by mid-level managers or compromised executives before reaching the independent board chair.

Retaliation Claims & SEC Bounty Exposure

Whistleblowers reporting externally to the SEC Whistleblower Office due to fear of workplace retaliation or lack of internal trust.

Inadequate Anti-Bribery (FCPA) Intermediary Controls

Failure to perform risk-based due diligence on third-party sales agents, consultants, and overseas joint venture partners.

Uncoordinated Independent Investigations

Investigations conducted without formal attorney-client privilege protocols, creating discoverable records that harm the company in future litigation.

Our Ethics & Investigation Architecture

A 4-phase integrity and escalation infrastructure protecting institutional culture and board independence:

Evaluating anonymous intake platforms, multi-language availability, and intake triage procedures for independence and confidentiality.

Establishing objective classification criteria that trigger mandatory, immediate notification to the Independent Audit Chair within 24 hours.

Conducting third-party due diligence reviews, gifts and hospitality policies, and forensic transaction testing in high-risk markets.

Structuring the governance charter for independent Special Investigation Committees, external counsel retention, and document preservation.

Key Deliverables

  • Institutional Whistleblower Intake & Anti-Retaliation Policy
  • Direct Board Audit Chair Escalation Tree & Severity Matrix
  • Comprehensive Anti-Bribery & Corruption (FCPA/UKBA) Compliance Manual
  • Third-Party Intermediary Due Diligence Protocol
  • Independent Special Investigation Board Committee Playbook
