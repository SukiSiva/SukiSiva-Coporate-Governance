Governance Policy Practice

Corporate Policy Development Practice

Drafting institutional employee codes of conduct, insider trading policies, conflict of interest procedures, and executive remuneration policies.

Practice Overview & Policy Architecture

Vague, outdated corporate policies create legal liability and regulatory exposure. Governora crafts institutional codes of conduct, insider trading pre-clearance policies, executive compensation clawback mechanisms, and conflict-of-interest protocols that are legally sound, operationally realistic, and plain-English accessible to global workforces.

Key Boardroom Challenges Addressed

Outdated Boilerplate Policy Manuals

Legacy policy manuals filled with legal jargon that employees ignore and that fail to reflect modern regulatory standards.

SEC Clawback Rule Non-Compliance (Rule 10D-1)

Failure to maintain mandatory, legally enforceable compensation clawback policies for erroneously awarded executive incentive pay.

Insider Trading & Rule 10b5-1 Plan Deficiencies

Unmonitored executive stock transactions and trading plans that fail to comply with strict SEC cooling-off period mandates.

Related-Party Transaction Blind Spots

Ambiguous conflict-of-interest definitions leading to unapproved vendor contracts with executive family members.

Our Policy Engineering Methodology

A comprehensive 4-phase policy lifecycle approach:

Reviewing all existing corporate policies against recent regulatory statutes, DOJ guidelines, and stock exchange listing rules.

Drafting modern, cohesive policies with clear operational examples, decision trees, and translated variations for global subsidiaries.

Establishing digital workflows for insider trading trade pre-clearance, gift/hospitality declarations, and conflict disclosures.

Structuring annual employee digital policy attestation tracking and mandatory executive compliance certification.
