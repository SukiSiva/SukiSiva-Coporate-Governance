Pre-IPO Software Governance & SOX 404 Readiness

Enterprise Cloud Software & SaaS 6-Month Mandate United States (SEC / NYSE Listing)

The Institutional Challenge

A high-growth enterprise SaaS platform preparing for an initial public offering (IPO) on the New York Stock Exchange discovered material weaknesses in financial reporting controls, segregation of duties in cloud access, and lack of an independent board committee structure.

Advisory Discovery & Gap Analysis

Governora was engaged as an independent special compliance advisor reporting directly to the Board Audit & Risk Committee. Our forensic teams identified three critical structural deficiencies:

  • Material Control Deficiencies: Revenue recognition under ASC 606 lacked automated control documentation across complex multi-element enterprise contracts.
  • Cloud Production Access Overlap: Software developers retained administrative production access to financial ledger databases without change-log approvals.
  • Immature Board Structure: The board operated without independent committee charters, formal nomination procedures, or an SEC-qualified Financial Expert.

The Remediation Blueprint & Execution

Governora orchestrated a comprehensive 12-month transformation protocol:

1. SOX 404 Internal Controls Architecture

Designed and deployed 140 key internal controls over financial reporting (ICFR), including automated access review workflows.

2. Board & Committee Formation

Recruited an independent audit committee chair meeting SEC financial expert criteria and drafted NYSE-compliant committee charters.

3. Cloud Security IT General Controls (ITGC)

Separated production access environments, enforced hardware-based MFA, and instituted automated change management tracking.

4. Mock SEC Review & Underwriter Due Diligence

Executed dry-run internal audits simulating auditor examinations and underwriter counsel legal scrutiny.
