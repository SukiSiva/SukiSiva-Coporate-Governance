Enterprise Technology Blueprint

Enterprise Technology Compliance Solutions

GDPR/CCPA global data governance, AI ethics board policies, SaaS vendor compliance auditing, and intellectual property protection.

Sector Overview & Supervisory Environment

Technology companies, cloud software providers, and AI innovators face aggressive regulatory headwinds ranging from European GDPR enforcement to the EU AI Act, FTC algorithmic fairness inquiries, and SEC cybersecurity disclosure mandates. Sustainable tech governance requires balancing rapid software deployment with defensible data ethics.

Key Regulatory & Compliance Challenges

Global Privacy & Cross-Border Data Transfers

Navigating Schrems II fallout, standard contractual clauses (SCCs), GDPR cross-border adequacy, and state-level US privacy laws (CCPA/CPRA).

AI Model Governance & Algorithmic Liability

Complying with the EU AI Act classification tiers, documenting model provenance, training data copyright clearance, and bias mitigation.

SaaS Vendor & Cloud Supply Chain Cascades

Auditing fourth-party vendor dependencies, SOC 2 Type II attestation gaps, and continuous sub-processor monitoring.

Intellectual Property & Trade Secret Fencing

Preventing internal code leakage into public large language models, patent integrity defense, and software licensing compliance.

Our Enterprise Technology Governance Methodology

A 4-stage governance architecture designed specifically for high-velocity software engineering and cloud environments:

Mapping enterprise telemetry pipelines, identifying sensitive data ingest points, and inventorying production AI models.

Drafting charters for board and executive AI ethics bodies, establishing algorithmic deployment go/no-go gates.

Automating vendor risk assessments, standardizing Data Processing Agreements (DPAs), and validating security attestations.

Conducting 72-hour GDPR supervisory notification simulations and SEC 4-day cyber materiality rehearsals.
