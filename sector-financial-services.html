Sector Advisory Blueprint

Financial Services Compliance Solutions

Basel III/IV capital adequacy, Dodd-Frank title compliance, AML/CFT risk assessments, and SEC broker-dealer oversight protocols.

Sector Overview & Supervisory Environment

Global financial institutions operate under unprecedented scrutiny from the Federal Reserve, OCC, SEC, FINRA, FCA, and ECB. With Basel III Endgame capital rules and stringent AML/CFT sanctions enforcement, financial firms require an integrated supervisory architecture that aligns treasury capital planning with day-to-day transaction monitoring and fiduciary compliance.

Key Regulatory & Compliance Challenges

Basel III/IV & Stress Testing

Calibrating risk-weighted assets (RWA), liquidity coverage ratios (LCR), and net stable funding ratios (NSFR) under severe macroeconomic stress scenarios.

AML/CFT & Sanctions Defensibility

Overcoming transaction monitoring backlogs, alert fatigue, and high-risk client intake silos across multi-jurisdictional correspondent banking.

Dodd-Frank & Broker-Dealer Rules

Maintaining strict compliance with swap dealer business conduct standards, trade reporting, and SEC net capital rules (Rule 15c3-1).

Off-Channel Communications Exposure

Remediating unapproved messaging platforms (WhatsApp, Signal) and ensuring complete recordkeeping under SEC Rule 17a-4.

Our Financial Services Regulatory Blueprint

We deploy a 4-phase regulatory readiness framework tailored to prudential banking standards and securities broker-dealer examinations:

Benchmarking CET1 capital ratios, liquidity buffers, and internal audit findings against supervisory exam manuals.

Conducting end-to-end model validation of transaction monitoring algorithms, PEP screening thresholds, and SAR escalation pathways.

Updating Written Supervisory Procedures, trade surveillance parameters, and conflict-of-interest information barriers.

Conducting mock regulatory interviews, testing examiner document production pipelines, and drafting defensible response memos.
