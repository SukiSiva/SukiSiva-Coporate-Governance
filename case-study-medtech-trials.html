EU MDR Medical Device Board Attestation & Audit

Medical Technology & In-Vitro Diagnostics 9-Month Mandate European Union (27 Member States) & UK (MHRA)

The Institutional Challenge

A commercial medical device manufacturer faced the imminent loss of European CE-mark certifications for 18 established clinical product lines due to severe bottleneck challenges under the strict new EU MDR requirements.

Advisory Discovery & Gap Analysis

Governora was engaged as an independent special compliance advisor reporting directly to the Board Audit & Risk Committee. Our forensic teams identified three critical structural deficiencies:

  • Inadequate Clinical Evidence Portfolios: Legacy devices approved under previous directives lacked continuous clinical data required under MDR Article 61.
  • Notified Body Communication Backlog: Disorganized technical submissions led to multiple rounds of questions and pending expiration deadlines.
  • Post-Market Clinical Follow-up Deficits: Post-market surveillance (PMS) procedures lacked auditable clinical follow-up data loops.

The Remediation Blueprint & Execution

Governora orchestrated a comprehensive 12-month transformation protocol:

1. Portfolio Recertification Command Center

Triaged 18 product families, mobilizing specialized clinical evidence generation teams.

2. Post-Market Surveillance Audit Trails

Instituted automated clinical registry tracking across 200 European hospitals to gather real-world performance metrics.

3. Unified Notified Body Submission Matrix

Standardized technical documentation dossiers, reducing review cycle times by 60%.

4. Board Regulatory Risk Oversight

Instituted executive board briefings tracking notified body audit findings and CE-mark renewal milestones.
