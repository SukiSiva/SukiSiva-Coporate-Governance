Regulatory Compliance Advisory Practice

Proactive alignment with SEC, FCA, ESMA, and statutory disclosure mandates. Gap analysis, enforcement remediation, and regulatory liaison.

Practice Overview & Regulatory Posture

Regulatory requirements are expanding globally, with supervisory agencies imposing multi-million-dollar fines and personal officer accountability for systemic non-compliance. Governora helps international enterprises anticipate statutory disclosure mandates, remediate supervisory examination deficiencies, and build audit-proof compliance programs.

Key Boardroom Challenges Addressed

Cross-Border Regulatory Fragmentation

Managing conflicting statutory requirements between US SEC disclosure rules, EU CSRD/CSDDD mandates, and UK FCA standards.

Enforcement Inquiries & Subpoena Surprises

Responding to formal supervisory inquiries or civil investigative demands (CIDs) without established internal investigation protocols.

Officer Certification Liability

Personal exposure for C-suite officers under Sarbanes-Oxley Sections 302 and 906 certification rules.

Third-Party Intermediary Risk

Regulatory exposure stemming from unmonitored overseas distributors, vendors, and cross-border joint venture partners.

Our Regulatory Alignment Methodology

A proactive 4-phase regulatory defense and attestation roadmap:

Cataloging statutory compliance mandates across every operating jurisdiction and subsidiary legal entity.

Comparing current policies and internal operational telemetry against regulator examination manuals and enforcement precedents.

Drafting institutional compliance manuals, supervisory procedures, and quantitative key compliance indicator (KCI) dashboards.

Conducting mock regulatory interviews with key officers to stress-test document production systems and examiner defense.
