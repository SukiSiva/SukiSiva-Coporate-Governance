Enterprise Risk Management Practice

Embedding COSO and ISO 31000 risk management frameworks, defining board risk appetite statements, and establishing early-warning metric dashboards.

Practice Overview & Enterprise Resilience

Traditional ERM often degenerates into static spreadsheets reviewed once a year by busy audit committees. Governora transforms risk management into an active, strategic boardroom asset. Grounded in COSO Enterprise Risk Management and ISO 31000, our frameworks quantify risk appetite, detect early-warning signals, and protect balance sheet solvency.

Key Boardroom Challenges Addressed

Siloed Risk Registers

Divisional business units tracking risks in isolation, blinding the executive committee to correlated, enterprise-wide compound risks.

Ambiguous Risk Appetite Statements

Vague corporate slogans ("we have zero tolerance for safety violations") that fail to provide quantifiable capital loss limits.

Neglect of Black Swan Scenarios

Over-reliance on historical probability distributions, leaving the board unprepared for geopolitical conflicts or cyber grid collapses.

Board-to-Risk Disconnection

Board risk committees lacking dedicated quantitative metrics and real-time early warning key risk indicators (KRIs).

Our Enterprise Risk Engineering Architecture

We build an active, telemetry-driven risk framework structured around four core phases:

Conducting executive risk discovery workshops to inventory top-tier strategic, financial, operational, and reputational hazards.

Establishing quantitative risk tolerance bands and capital loss thresholds tied directly to corporate credit ratings and debt covenants.

Constructing automated Key Risk Indicator (KRI) data pipelines with early-warning threshold triggers for executive escalation.

Drafting Risk Committee oversight calendars and conducting annual severe macroeconomic stress-testing scenarios.

Key Deliverables

  • Quantified Board Risk Appetite Statement & Tolerance Matrix
  • Executive Key Risk Indicator (KRI) Dashboard Specification

Frequently Asked Questions

Our methodology integrates all five components of COSO ERM: Governance & Culture, Strategy & Objective-Setting, Performance, Review, and Information & Reporting.
